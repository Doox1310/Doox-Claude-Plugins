# Metric dictionary — K01–K56

Tra cứu theo dòng, dùng khi đặt hai con số cạnh nhau. Bảng này **không chứa số liệu thị trường nào** —
chỉ định nghĩa, đơn vị, phạm vi bắt buộc phải nêu kèm, và lý do hai con số trông giống nhau lại không
so được với nhau.

Ba luật dùng bảng:

- Nguồn có định nghĩa riêng khác định nghĩa ở đây → **giữ định nghĩa của nguồn**, và hòa giải trước
  khi so sánh (`research-method` §11).
- Mọi quy đổi là claim `Ước tính`, phải ghi công thức, đầu vào và giả định.
- `required_scope` thiếu thì con số chưa dùng được: một chỉ số không kèm phạm vi/kỳ/đơn vị là
  `Chưa xác minh`, không phải một con số yếu.

## Metric definitions for comparable evidence

_Use only relevant measures. These definitions contain no market values or company actuals._

| metric_id | metric | definition | unit | required_scope | comparison_caution | source_locator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K01 | Market trips | Completed trips within the defined market and period. | trips/period | Geography, service category, period. | Requests, registrations and completed trips are different. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A7:F7 |
| K02 | Gross bookings / GMV | Total transaction value according to the original source definition. | currency/period | Tax, promotions, cancellations, refunds and service scope. | Do not add GMV to operator revenue. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A8:F8 |
| K03 | Operator revenue | Revenue recognized by the entity on its disclosed accounting basis. | currency/period | Entity, principal/agent basis, period and included services. | Customer payments may include pass-through amounts. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A9:F9 |
| K04 | Market share | Entity measure divided by total market measure on the same basis. | % | Trips, bookings, revenue or users; same geography and period. | User share cannot substitute for trip share. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A10:F10 |
| K05 | Active fleet | Vehicles meeting the source activity definition within the period. | vehicles | Activity threshold, period and location. | Separate owned, ordered, delivered, registered and active vehicles. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A11:F11 |
| K06 | Active drivers | Distinct drivers meeting the stated activity threshold. | drivers | Period, geography, identity basis and multi-app participation. | Adding platform counts may double-count drivers. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A12:F12 |
| K07 | Average trip distance | Passenger-carrying distance divided by completed trips. | km/trip | Service class, location, period and included trip legs. | Airport, urban and intercity mixes are not interchangeable. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A13:F13 |
| K08 | Total daily mileage | Total distance divided by defined vehicle-days. | km/vehicle-day | Paid/empty distance and active/calendar day basis. | Passenger distance alone omits empty travel. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A14:F14 |
| K09 | Paid-km share | Passenger-carrying km divided by total vehicle km. | % | Same fleet, period and distance boundary. | This is not time utilization. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A15:F15 |
| K10 | Time utilization | Passenger-carrying time divided by explicitly defined available time. | % | Clock, service or online time; matching sample and period. | Do not compare sources with different availability denominators. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A16:F16 |
| K11 | Trips per vehicle-day | Completed trips divided by defined vehicle-days. | trips/vehicle-day | Active versus calendar days, service and fleet sample. | Disclose duty cycle and vehicle count. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A17:F17 |
| K12 | Customer fare per trip | Customer trip payment including the stated fees, taxes and promotions. | currency/trip | Route, time, category, account eligibility and channel. | Upfront quote and final paid fare are distinct. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A18:F18 |
| K13 | Fare per paid km | Defined fare amount divided by passenger-carrying distance. | currency/paid-km | Base/time charges, minimum, tax, tolls and promotion treatment. | Convert denominators before comparing with cost per total km. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A19:F19 |
| K14 | Take rate | Amount retained by platform/operator divided by defined transaction value. | % | Retained fees, taxes, subsidies and denominator. | Advertised commission is not necessarily realized take rate. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A20:F20 |
| K15 | Driver net income | External earnings after all driver-borne costs included in the study. | currency/hour or day | Hours, fees, rent, energy, insurance and tax assumptions. | Maximum advertised income is not typical net income. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A21:F21 |
| K16 | EV energy consumption | Electric energy consumed divided by distance, at a stated measurement boundary. | kWh/km | Battery/grid measurement, weather, load, route and model. | Do not add charging losses twice. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A22:F22 |
| K17 | ICE fuel consumption | Fuel volume used over distance under defined conditions. | litres/100km | Fuel grade, vehicle, load and duty cycle. | Convert litres/100km consistently when deriving cost/km. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A23:F23 |
| K18 | Effective electricity cost | Included electricity charges divided by electricity at the stated meter boundary. | currency/kWh | Energy, demand, fixed fees, reactive charges and taxes. | Energy-only tariff is not the complete effective cost. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A24:F24 |
| K19 | Public charging price | Total charging-service charges under the applicable CPO tariff. | currency/kWh or session | Energy/time fees, idle, parking, subscription and taxes. | Do not substitute a utility tariff for CPO pricing. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A25:F25 |
| K20 | Charging site | One physical location hosting charging equipment. | sites | Location, operational status and access category. | A site is not a charger or connector. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A26:F26 |
| K21 | Charging connector | Defined charging outlet, with simultaneous service capability stated. | connectors | Connector type, status and shared output constraints. | Two connectors may not serve two vehicles simultaneously. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A27:F27 |
| K22 | Charging power | Rated, available or average charging power, identified separately. | kW | AC/DC, vehicle acceptance, shared supply and SOC. | Summed ratings do not establish usable concurrent power. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A28:F28 |
| K23 | Charging energy | Electricity delivered to vehicles or purchased at the grid during the period. | kWh/period | Meter boundary, losses, sessions and period. | Power in kW and energy in kWh are different. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A29:F29 |
| K24 | Charging availability | Usable time divided by defined service time for the stated unit. | % | Site/connector scope, exclusions, maintenance and monitoring. | Backend online status does not establish successful charging. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A30:F30 |
| K25 | Charge success rate | Successful attempts or sessions divided by the stated attempt/session denominator. | % | Success definition, retry treatment, sample and period. | Uptime and successful-start rate are not interchangeable. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A31:F31 |
| K26 | Mean time to restore | Average elapsed time between defined incident start and restored service. | hours/event | Start/end events, exclusions, sample and service. | Response, repair and restoration time must remain distinct. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A32:F32 |
| K27 | Queue time | Elapsed waiting time before access to the specified service. | minutes | Charging/pickup service, place, time band, percentile and sample. | Means hide long-tail waits. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A33:F33 |
| K28 | Vehicle landed cost | Cost to deliver a vehicle within the explicitly included scope. | currency/vehicle | Model, battery, freight, duties, taxes and registration. | Do not assume ready-for-service costs are included. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A34:F34 |
| K29 | Maintenance cost | Defined maintenance and repair expense over the stated exposure. | currency/km or year | Planned/unplanned, tires, warranty, age and distance. | Match scope before comparing EV and ICE. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A35:F35 |
| K30 | External illustrative TCO | Lifecycle costs from external inputs and hypothetical assumptions divided by defined distance. | currency/total-km | Horizon, residual, finance, tax, infrastructure and cost boundary. | This is not company actual cost or a company forecast. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A36:F36 |
| K31 | External illustrative contribution | Defined external/hypothetical revenue less scoped variable costs. | currency/km or trip | Matching revenue/cost denominator and tax treatment. | Contribution is not net profit; include empty distance consistently. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A37:F37 |
| K32 | Illustrative break-even threshold | Hypothetical activity level covering the explicitly scoped costs. | trips/day or km/day | External price/cost inputs, capacity constraints and scenario assumptions. | This is not a validated company threshold. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A38:F38 |
| K33 | Lead time | Duration between explicitly defined start and completion milestones. | days or weeks | Statutory, quoted or observed; calendar/business days. | Do not sum parallel activities as if sequential. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A39:F39 |
| K34 | Quoted price | Price offered for a specified scope under current stated conditions. | currency/unit | Model, quantity, taxes, currency, delivery terms and expiry. | A benchmark or expired list price is not a current quote. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A40:F40 |
| K35 | Effective financing cost | Funding cost including stated interest, fees and compulsory charges. | annual % or currency | Rate basis, compounding, fee timing, principal and currency. | Flat rate differs from reducing-balance rate and effective APR. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A41:F41 |
| K36 | Battery retention | Measured remaining capacity relative to a defined initial capacity. | % | Age, cycles, km, chemistry, temperature and test method. | Dashboard SoH can differ from independent measured capacity. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A42:F42 |
| K37 | Customer rating / NPS | Original survey or review score; NPS is promoters share minus detractors share under its method. | score or percentage points | Question, scale, sampling, period and respondent group. | A review-star rating is not NPS; do not combine the two. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A43:F43 |
| K38 | Demand growth | Change in the same demand measure relative to a comparable prior period. | % | Volume/value, real/nominal, geography and measurement method. | A methodology change is not organic growth. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A44:F44 |
| K39 | Booking acceptance rate | Accepted requests divided by eligible requests under the source definition. | % | Broadcast/retry handling, service, time and request population. | Do not confuse driver-level offers with unique customer bookings. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A45:F45 |
| K40 | Booking cancellation rate | Cancelled bookings divided by the stated request or accepted-booking population. | % | Cancelling party, stage, retries, no-shows and period. | Different denominators create different rates. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A46:F46 |
| K41 | Trip completion rate | Completed trips divided by explicitly defined requests or accepted bookings. | % | Matching booking population, period and exclusions. | Completion after acceptance is not completion of all requests. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A47:F47 |
| K42 | Passenger pickup wait | Time from the defined booking/acceptance event to passenger pickup. | minutes | Start event, completed/cancelled sample, time band and percentile. | Quoted ETA and actual pickup wait differ; cancelled waits may be excluded. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A48:F48 |
| K43 | External customer acquisition cost | Scoped acquisition spend divided by defined newly acquired customers in an external study. | currency/new customer | Paid/blended spend, acquisition event, cohort and attribution window. | Registrations are not necessarily paying riders; never infer internal CAC. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A49:F49 |
| K44 | External retention / repeat use | Share of an original external cohort meeting the stated repeat-use condition. | % | Cohort start, qualifying trip, horizon, sample and activity definition. | Monthly active users do not establish retention of the same cohort. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A50:F50 |
| K45 | Support response time | Elapsed time from customer contact to the defined first response. | minutes or hours | Channel, business/clock time, automation and percentile. | An automated acknowledgement is not necessarily a substantive response. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A51:F51 |
| K46 | Support resolution rate/time | Resolved cases share or elapsed resolution time, kept as separate measures. | % or hours | Case population, closure criteria, reopening and observation window. | Closed tickets do not automatically represent customer resolution. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A52:F52 |
| K47 | Complaint incidence | Relevant external complaints divided by defined service exposure. | complaints/100000 trips | Complaint definition, source coverage, severity and matching trip denominator. | Raw review counts cannot establish complaint rates. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A53:F53 |
| K48 | Safety incident rate | Defined incidents divided by comparable exposure, with severity separated. | incidents/1000000 km or trips | Incident type, severity, reporting coverage, geography and period. | Do not compare distance-based and trip-based rates without conversion evidence. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A54:F54 |
| K49 | Settlement delay | Elapsed time between a defined transaction event and funds becoming available. | business/calendar days | Provider, instrument, risk holds, reserves and payout eligibility. | Advertised T+1 may exclude holds, weekends and failed payouts. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A55:F55 |
| K50 | Chargeback rate | Chargebacks divided by a specified transaction-count or transaction-value base. | % | Instrument, dispute timing, count/value basis and reporting window. | Count-based and value-based rates are different; match event cohorts. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A56:F56 |
| K51 | External fraud-loss rate | Verified fraud losses divided by the externally defined transaction exposure. | % or basis points | Gross/net recovery, fraud type, value base and observation method. | Vendor fraud-attempt claims are not realized loss or company leakage. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A57:F57 |
| K52 | Residual value retention | Realized comparable resale value divided by comparable original vehicle cost. | % or currency/vehicle | Model, age, mileage, condition, battery, taxes and sale channel. | Asking prices and conditional guarantees are not realized sale values. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A58:F58 |
| K53 | Total compensation benchmark | External pay and stated employer/worker benefits for a comparable role and period. | currency/year or hour | Location, seniority, hours, benefits, taxes and employment model. | Base salary cannot be compared directly with all-in compensation. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A59:F59 |
| K54 | External staff turnover | Departures divided by the source-defined workforce population over a period. | %/period | Voluntary/involuntary, average/headcount base, roles and survey method. | Sector turnover does not diagnose company retention. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A60:F60 |
| K55 | Vehicle service availability | Time or vehicle-days available for passenger service divided by the stated fleet exposure. | % | Fleet population, planned downtime, driver availability and time basis. | Vehicle availability, utilization and active-fleet share are distinct. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A61:F61 |
| K56 | Lifecycle emissions intensity | Scoped greenhouse-gas emissions divided by vehicle-km or passenger-km. | gCO2e/vehicle-km or passenger-km | Manufacture/use/end-of-life boundary, grid, lifetime mileage and occupancy. | Tailpipe-only comparisons cannot support lifecycle or climate-neutral claims. | 02_METRIC_DEFINITIONS!A62:F62 |
